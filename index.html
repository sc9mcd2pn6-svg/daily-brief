🚢Hormuz — Crisis

Hormuz talks stall — Trump halted strike but Oman mediation shows no breakthrough

President Trump called off a planned military strike on Iran over the weekend but Oman-mediated talks have produced no agreement on reopening the Strait of Hormuz. Iran says negotiations are in “final stages” but insists it will never return to pre-war passage terms, aiming for a 1–3 month arrangement that leaves Tehran in control. Trump publicly branded Iranian leadership “unbelievably duplicitous” after Tehran contradicted his claim of a deal “coming.” Oil is holding above $90/barrel; roughly 20% of global oil normally transits the Strait.

Al Jazeera · CNN · Wikipedia (2026 Strait of Hormuz Crisis)
Why it matters to you

Oil above $90 and Hormuz blocked means war-risk premiums on equipment shipments into both Lebanon and Nigeria remain elevated; factor this into your project pricing and insurance on any Lagos or Beirut contracts you are quoting now. A protracted stalemate (the most likely scenario) keeps costs structurally high through Q4.

🏘️ Real Estate
🏗️Data Centre RE — Market

Global data-centre real estate hits $87 billion — AI demand drives 13% annual growth, $1.2 trillion build-out ahead

The global data-centre real estate market is valued at $87.17 billion in 2026, up 13.4% year-on-year from $76.88 billion, and is projected to reach $143 billion by 2030. Q1 2026 data shows Latin America leading inventory growth at +41.3%, with North America at +33%, both driven by hyperscale AI requirements. CBRE, JLL, and PGIM all rate data centres as the most competitive and liquid commercial real-estate asset class of the current cycle; analysts project 100 GW of new capacity over five years, representing ~$1.2 trillion in real-estate asset value and $870 billion in new debt financing.

CBRE Global Data Center Trends 2026 · JLL Research · Research and Markets
Why it matters to you

The institutional consensus is now unmistakable: data-centre infrastructure is the decade’s prime real-estate asset class. Lagos sits at the frontier of that wave in West Africa — the capital chasing these assets globally will eventually land there, and your fire-suppression and security positioning at the ground floor is the right play right now.

🌍 Africa & Nigeria
⚡Lagos · Data Centres

Lagos data-centre wave accelerates — $240M OADC campus, Digital Realty third site, Kasi Cloud 100MW AI campus all in motion

Open Access Data Centres (OADC) has announced a $240 million, 24 MW facility in Lagos; Digital Realty is building a third Lagos data centre deepening its West Africa footprint; Equinix’s LG3 (610 sq. m) is live as the first phase of a $100 million Africa expansion; and Kasi Cloud’s 100 MW AI-focused hyperscale campus across 42 hectares is under construction — targeting high-density GPU workloads. The Nigeria data-centre market is valued at $374 million in 2026, with Lagos holding roughly 30% of national market share. Power reliability, not capital, remains the single binding constraint: operators are increasingly going off-grid with on-site generation.

🇱🇧 Lebanon
🏦Economy

IIF locks in 12% GDP contraction — all IMF aid explicitly tied to Hezbollah disarmament

The Institute of International Finance has finalised its 2026 Lebanon GDP forecast at a 12% real contraction, reversing 2025’s modest 5% growth as tourism has collapsed, reconstruction inflows have stalled, and daily Israeli strikes continue. Lebanon’s economy minister has publicly confirmed that IMF and World Bank recovery financing remains explicitly conditional on disarmament of non-state armed groups and expanded army deployment in the south — conditions that have not advanced. Foreign reserves hold near $11.6 billion.

🔥France — August

Electricity bills up 2.5%, Livret A raised to 1.7%, wildfires intensifying — fuel aid extended to August 31

From August 1, regulated electricity tariffs in France rose ~2.5% (~€26/year) due to higher network usage fees; gas prices fell slightly (-0.8%). The Livret A savings rate was simultaneously raised from 1.5% to 1.7%. A severe wildfire season is intensifying across southern France, expected to persist through summer. A €100 fuel aid scheme for drivers has been extended through August 31 after its July 31 deadline was missed by many applicants. A partial solar eclipse covering up to 96% of the disk in some regions is forecast for August 12.
