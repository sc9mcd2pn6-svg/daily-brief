Rome 7th-round talks deadlocked over Ali al-Taher Ridge; IED kills 2 Israeli reservists, IDF strikes south Lebanon

The seventh round of US-mediated Israel–Lebanon talks in Rome ended this week without a breakthrough. Israel is demanding full removal of Hezbollah’s underground command infrastructure at the Ali al-Taher Ridge near Nabatieh as a precondition for further withdrawals; Lebanon’s President Aoun proposed transferring the tunnel complex to the Lebanese army, which Hezbollah has rejected. Prime Beirut fresh-dollar yields hold at 5–7% as transaction volumes sink 29% — a thin market with upside deferred

Reconstruction demand — the eventual catalyst for capital-value recovery — will not materialise until a durable ceasefire and international financing are confirmed.
