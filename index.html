US Approves Its First Small Modular Nuclear Reactor — 300 MW Plant Greenlit in Tennessee

The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) issued a construction permit on September 29 for the Tennessee Valley Authority to build a BWRX-300 small modular reactor (SMR) at its Clinch River site in Oak Ridge, Tennessee — only the second commercial nuclear construction permit issued in the United States in decades. The 300-megawatt GE Vernova Hitachi boiling-water design passed its comprehensive NRC safety review in 14 months, a record pace. TVA will need a separate operating licence before loading fuel and beginning operations.

The approval is a milestone for the global SMR industry, which has seen multiple projects cancelled or delayed due to cost overruns (including NuScale’s high-profile collapse in 2023). A successful Tennessee build would validate the economics of factory-built modular reactors and potentially unlock a generation of projects: tech giants including Microsoft, Google and Amazon have been exploring SMR contracts to provide always-on, carbon-free power for hyperscale data centres, where grid reliability is critical.
