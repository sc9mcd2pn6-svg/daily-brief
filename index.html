France’s great Gironde fire finally stable — but summer costs mount

A massive blaze in the Gironde near Bordeaux — at 42,000 hectares one of the largest of the season — has been stable since August 2, with most of the 200,000 evacuees now returning home, though some areas still burn. Fires also continue in the Var department. Total wildfire and extreme-heat damage across France, Spain and southern Europe has reached €3.1 billion this season. France’s regulated electricity bills also rise 2.5% (about €26/year) from this month due to grid investment charges.

Lebanon’s economy on track for 12% contraction — IIF issues stark warning
