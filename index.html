Macron heads into the highest-stakes budget fight of his presidency

The final budget showdown of Emmanuel Macron’s presidency is shaping up as France’s biggest fiscal clash of the decade. The prime minister and finance minister used the country’s largest annual economic gathering to stress the urgency of curbing a ballooning debt burden. His second term has hardened into structural deadlock; growth should recover modestly as inflation eases, but agencies warn weak fiscal consolidation and gridlock are now permanent features.
