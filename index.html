🌍 World
🚢Hormuz — Breaking

Cargo ship struck in the Strait of Hormuz — crew abandons vessel, one missing

A dry bulk carrier was hit by an unknown projectile before dawn off Oman in the Strait of Hormuz, forcing the crew to abandon ship with one seafarer missing — the most direct commercial casualty in weeks. The attack came as US and Iranian officials gave flatly contradictory accounts of diplomatic progress: Treasury Secretary Bessent said a deal to reopen the strait was “coming,” while Trump called Iranian leadership “unbelievably duplicitous” and Iran said it is talking only through Oman. The strait has been effectively blockaded since February; roughly 20% of global oil transits it.

AP · KSAT · Al Jazeera · NBC News
Why it matters to you

A vessel actually struck is a step-change from general disruption: war-risk insurance premiums on equipment shipments into Lebanon and Nigeria will spike, and client capital decisions — including data-centre builds in Lagos — may pause again until the diplomatic read clarifies.

🇺🇦Ukraine

Ukrainian drone kills 7 on Russian beach — children among dead as Black Sea escalation deepens

A Ukrainian drone struck a crowded beach in southwestern Russia today, killing seven civilians including three children and wounding forty. Russia separately claimed it targeted Ukrainian cargo ships in the Black Sea. The dual maritime front — Hormuz and the Black Sea simultaneously disrupted — is the sharpest combined shipping shock since the war began, with reinsurers now pricing in worst-case scenarios on both corridors.

🏘️ Real Estate
🏗️Megadeal — Breaking

Prologis acquires SEGRO for £14 billion — biggest industrial real-estate deal ever, with data-centre core

Prologis, the world’s largest logistics REIT, announced a recommended all-share takeover of SEGRO plc — Europe’s biggest industrial and data-centre landlord — at approximately £14 billion ($18.8 billion). The combined group will control 117 million sq ft of space spanning European logistics and data-centre campuses (London, Paris, Warsaw). Prologis will seek a secondary LSE listing; deal is expected to close H1 2027 pending shareholder and regulatory approval. Analysts call it the defining transaction of the 2026 global industrial property cycle.

Bloomberg · CRE Daily · PR Newswire · Morningstar
Why it matters to you

The world’s largest logistics player betting £14 billion on data-centre real estate is the clearest market signal yet that hyperscale infrastructure is the decade’s prime asset class — Lagos is the West-Africa version of that thesis, and this deal will accelerate institutional capital flowing into African data-centre builds that need your fire and security expertise.

🌍 Africa & Nigeria
⚡Lagos · Data Centres

Lagos operators go fully off-grid — Tetracore builds 100 MW on-site gas plant as grid reliability collapses

The defining story in Lagos data-centre construction today is not new openings but power independence: the Tetracore Atakobo campus is bypassing the national grid entirely with a 100 MW on-site gas generation plant, a model other operators are now studying. Airtel/Nxtra’s 38 MW hyperscale campus is expected operational by year-end; Kasi Cloud’s 100 MW AI-focused campus (42 hectares) is under construction. Industry sources confirm that power reliability — not capital or land — is now the single binding constraint across all Lagos builds.

Techmoonshot · Data Center Dynamics · Technext24
Why it matters to you

Off-grid gas plants dramatically raise on-site fire risk: fuel storage, generator halls, and uninterruptible power infrastructure all require specialist suppression systems that many local contractors cannot deliver — a direct differentiator for Quasar Nigeria if you position now before tier-one integrators lock preferred-vendor status.

🇱🇧 Lebanon
🏦Economy

IIF locks in 12% GDP contraction — economy minister says all aid tied to Hezbollah disarmament

The Institute of International Finance finalised its 2026 Lebanon forecast at a 12% real GDP contraction, reversing last year’s 5% growth as tourism has collapsed and reconstruction inflows have stalled. Lebanon’s economy minister confirmed publicly today that recovery financing from the IMF and World Bank remains explicitly conditional on disarmament of non-state armed groups and expanded army deployment in the south — conditions that have not advanced. Reserves hold near $11.6 billion; daily Israeli strikes continue.

IIF · BLOMINVEST · Al Jazeera · Barchart
Why it matters to you

The minister’s public linkage of aid to disarmament is a new hard signal: no disarmament progress = no IMF disbursement = no broad credit recovery for Lebanese clients. Keep FGC/Quasar Lebanon contracts front-loaded in fresh dollars and avoid receivables that stretch beyond 60 days.
