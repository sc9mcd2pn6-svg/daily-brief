Crypto / Cyber

Binance suffers $6.3 billion heist — biggest crypto theft in history, attributed to North Korea

Hackers infiltrated Binance, the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange, and stole an estimated $6.3 billion in what appears to be the single largest crypto heist ever recorded. Multiple blockchain analytics firms attributed the attack to North Korea’s Lazarus Group. Binance suspended deposits and withdrawals after discovering the breach and said it would cover all customer losses; it coordinated with other exchanges to freeze approximately $3 billion of the stolen assets before they could be laundered.

The New York Times notes that risks to crypto are growing sharper as attackers use AI and other advanced techniques both to steal funds and to launder them at speed. The breach will intensify regulatory pressure on centralised exchanges globally and may accelerate institutional moves toward self-custody and decentralised settlement.
